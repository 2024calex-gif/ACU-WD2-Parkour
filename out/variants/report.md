## What is measured

- Window penetration and touch: bake2 validation, non-finger body incl. feet over [o, span end]. PASS as in bake2: penetration <= 5 cm, touch >= 4 frames per hand, Delta step <= 3 cm/frame.
- Entry step: largest angle between consecutive 60 Hz samples of any patched bone over frame pairs in [o, o + 0.10 s] (what the game plays from o). Exit step: the same over [o + window - 0.10 s, o + window + 0.25 s].
- Root step: change of (Reference position - original) per 60 Hz frame in the same windows.
- Game gap at o: our pose minus the original pose at o (the game blends from the previous clip, which matches the original entry, into ours). Game gap at o + window: the same at o + window. Max gap over [o + window, o + window + 0.25 s]: the difference from the original pose while the game blends into the next clip.
- Tail pen: non-finger penetration after o + window (for tail orig it is the ACU original).
- The original row is the unpatched clip: its own steps and penetration in the same windows.

## Conclusions (data only; not checked in game)

- Hand touch: the 0.20 s fade-in is what removes the hand touch on three clips (Fd010cm-Up100cm, Fd200cm-Up150cm, Fd200cm-Up050cm: 0 frames, need 4). Every variant with a fade-in of 0.08 s or none (B, C, D) has no touch failure. Fd010cm-Up100cm, enabled in out\patches\mapping.json, passes the touch and penetration checks under B, C and D but fails under A.
- Game gap at the entry (our pose minus the original pose at o): A 0.2 deg, B and D 0.4 to 1.2 deg, C 78 to 106 deg. With no entry fade the WD2 pose at o is a different stride phase from the ACU pose the game blends from (arms and legs 50 to 100 deg apart at o, per-bone check on Fd100cm-Up100cm).
- Game gap over o + window .. o + window + 0.25 s (where the game blends into the next clip): A 0 to 0.2 deg, B 0 to 1.0 deg, C and D 65 to 165 deg. The wd2 tail keeps the WD2 motion, which is far from the ACU pose after the window.
- Joint speed: the peak per-frame step at the entry is 21 deg (A), 38 deg (B, D), 21 deg (C); at the exit 30 (A), 39 (B), 25 (C, D). A shorter fade-in makes the entry faster.
- Penetration in the window (PASS metric, max over clips): A 17.7 cm, B 8.5, C 1.8, D 5.0. Penetration after the window (not in PASS): 4.9 cm for A and B; up to 40.7 cm for C and D (Fd010cm-Up200cm), because the envelopes end at the window and the WD2 motion after it is raw.
- PASS count (bake2 criteria): A 1 of 10, B 2, C 3, D 3. The Delta step (3 cm per frame) is the criterion most often failed by every variant. The one-frame need spikes come from the blended WD2 pose (for example 58 cm on Fd300cm-Up150cm under B, 7.5 cm on Fd100cm-Up100cm), and tall walls need 85 to 99 cm of lift (Fd010cm-Up200cm), which is pre-existing in T9e.

Smoothest, from these numbers: B_short. It is the only variant that fixes the touch while keeping the game gap at both blends under about 1 deg, and it has the lowest window penetration of the variants with an orig tail. Its cost is a faster entry and exit (38 to 39 deg per frame against 21 to 30 for A). C_nofade_wd2tail has the lowest penetration and lowest exit speed, but the game would have to bridge about 105 deg at the entry and 165 deg at the exit, and its tail penetrates up to 41 cm. I would test A_current against B_short first and keep C and D as a secondary test.

Limits: the game's own blend lengths are not in the data, so the gaps measure what the game blends from, not the blend itself. The next clip's start pose is not known, so the exit gap is measured against the original clip's pose after the window.
