## Criteria and what they measure

- window / blend penetration: bake2.penetration_parts on the non-finger body, to the cut and to the cut + 0.25 s (limit 5 cm).
- hand touch: planting hand within 5 cm of its target, consecutive plant frames from the first (need 4).
- Delta step: largest change of the Reference z offset between 60 Hz frames (limit 3 cm).
- trunk tilt: angle of Spine2 - Hips from vertical over the window (limit 65 deg).
- foot skate: p95 horizontal speed of the lowest foot over stance frames (height <= 0.05 m and |vz| <= 0.3 m/s), from the ACU landing to cut + blend (limit 0.5 m/s). The same rule on the original clip is shown for comparison.
- continuity: per patched bone, the angle between the pose at the cut (patch, or original) and the first pose of xx_Parkour_Muraille_Exit_Fd100cm-Dn100cm (median / max).
- jaggedness: largest per-60 Hz-frame bone angle step (bake2.jaggedness_t9e): patch, retargeted source, original.
- landed before the cut: ACU feet down (clip s) <= cut.

- xx_Parkour_Sprint_SlideOver_Fd100cm-Up100cm: FAILED offline (Delta step 3.7 cm/frame; foot skate p95 2.31 m/s). Written with enabled = true and the override below.
