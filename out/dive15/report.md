## 6. Grounded?

* Roll: the numbers say grounded (p95 0.54, max 0.88 m/s). The clearance itself is not held within its 2 cm band (about 44 percent of post-plant samples are outside it). The sheet shows the SlideOver feet and hips low at the floor through the roll with no sliding trail of the body, so it reads as grounded, with the caveat that the clearance deviation is still 10 cm p95 (section 3).
* Get-up: not grounded. The lowest joint moves at 5 to 6 m/s (Exit window p95 5.98) and the Exit trails in the sheet jitter along the floor. The locks reduce the feet to the pinned targets only where they are fitted: the ankle misses its pin by 8 to 24 cm (over-extension), and the pins do not cover the hands or the upper body that are the lowest joint there.
* Slowdown experiment (run once, not kept as output): slowing the Exit root inside the lock spans (alphas 0.2 to 0.8) made the pin error larger (30 to 51 cm) and the get-up skate larger (max 6.9 to 15.8 m/s), and the floor fell to -3.5 cm. The slow-down direction is therefore wrong for these pins; the output keeps no slowdown (MAX_SLOW_ITERS = 0).
