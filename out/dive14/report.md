## 6. What limits the skate (diagnosis)

* Roll (clip 1.37 to 1.45 s, WD2 1.20 to 1.28 s): the chain lowers the body by up to 0.15 m (solved contact lift from dive9, range -0.15 to +0.16 m across the window). At 1.40 s the pelvis sits at 0.11 m, where WD2's pelvis is at 0.25 m with its lowest point (a foot) 8 cm up. The upper leg (LeftUpLeg, 2 to 5 cm up) then moves with the pelvis at 2.4 to 2.8 m/s: this is the roll p95 and max. The Hips follow the velocity-domain target exactly (world Hips y equals the target, difference 0.000 m at every frame).
* Plant: the WD2 reconstruction itself leaves the plant hands at z = 0.06 to 0.10 m (right hand -0.04 m) with 0.5 to 0.9 m/s hand speed (solver residual plant_z 0.061 m rms), so the native plant is 0.88 m/s p95. The ACU plant hands are pinned by the hand IK, so the ACU plant (0.68 m/s p95) is less affected.
* Get-up (Exit played 0 to 0.42 s): the lowest joint moves at 5 to 8.6 m/s, while the root is 2.9 to 4.5 m/s. The rest is the Exit clip's own stand-up rotation and the Reference carried from W (the Exit Reference is carried, not solved). The root alone cannot reduce it. Holding the Exit root near zero until the joint leaves the floor would reduce it but breaks the speed rule (root >= 0 with v1 at Nav_Run) and the landing spot; not done here.
* What would be needed: (a) the roll contact lift must not lower the pelvis below the WD2 path (a change to dive9's contact solve, which has defaults I was asked not to change), or (b) a foot / thigh contact solve that keeps the upper leg off the floor, or (c) an Exit root that follows the contact point. Each is a decision for you; none was made here.
* Nothing was run in game (rule). The skate numbers are the offline model; the in-game check is still to do.
