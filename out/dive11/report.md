## 6. Decisions and caveats

* Foot IK aims at the envelope target (2 cm), not the 1 cm of the brief. With a 1 cm IK target, the Exit lift re-raises every toe between 1 and 2 cm, which is the body lift the IK is meant to avoid.
* The SlideOver lift is one-sided and runs as a few one-shot passes, not an iteration. dive9's two-sided ground contact has no fixed point in the vault over the box (the body is held up by its hands): it swings the lift +41 cm and -25 cm within 0.2 s and never settles. The new lift starts from dive9's and only raises; dive9's rate floor is kept.
* The plant (hands pinned by the hand IK) and the 6 frames before it get the floor only (target 0.5 cm, no box clearance there). A raise in that window moves the hands off their ground plant, and the hand touch fails (1 to 2 frames, need 3). With this rule the touch is 4 frames.
* The floor rule holds in every phase (minimum 0.29 cm, RightHand at 0.92 s). The +2 cm aim is met at the roll end (2.00 cm), in the Exit window (1.96 cm) and the Exit tail (2.00 cm). The pre-plant right hand and the plant forearm sit at the floor-only target, as above.
* The model's floor is not the game's floor. In game the roll-end hand reaches -8 to -12.6 cm and the toes 2 to 12 cm under the ground; the model gave -2.5 cm (roll-end hand) and -3.4 cm (Exit toe) before the fix. The fix is in the model's ground (z = 0). It needs an in-game check.
* Failures present before and after (dive9 and dive10 items, not changed here): world pelvis dv 10.87 / head 13.16 m/s per frame (> 1.5), world step 32.4 / 27.6 cm (> 12), Reference offset 1.91 m (> 1.0). The same values are in delay15.
* Nav gap at the window end: the left foot is 19.6 deg (delay15 3.8), inside the 25 deg rule; the IK turns the foot.
* Aux tracks: rebuilt on the new Reference, feet free until clip frame 13 (delay15: 13).
