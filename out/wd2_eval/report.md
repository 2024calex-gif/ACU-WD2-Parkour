## Method notes and caveats

- Flight = takeoff (end of the last foot contact before the first hand plant) to landing (start of the first foot contact after it). Without a hand plant it is the largest gap between foot contacts (>= 0.2 s). Contacts are the candidate runs of wd2events: a foot or hand moving backward within 35 % of the run-in body speed for >= 0.1 s.
- Event accuracy on the hand-annotated references: takeoff and landing within 0.03 s. The hand plant start is within 0.02 s for the kong but 0.25 s early for the frontdive (an arm sweep matches the speed band). The kong plant end is 0.15 s early: the body decelerates while the hands are down, so the band loses them.
- Pelvis path: one least-squares problem on a 60 Hz grid. Foot contacts before takeoff and after landing pin the pelvis height (ground) and xy; hand contacts in the flight have a free surface height; inside the flight the pelvis is ballistic (second difference (0, 0, -g) dt^2) on frames without contact; origin at takeoff. Candidate ground runs whose implied height is more than 6 cm off are dropped (up to 3 passes; listed as dropped runs).
- What the path identifies: the forward travel (pinned by the feet before takeoff and after landing), the standing hips height, and the hips height at the ground contacts. Between the contacts it is not identified (the path drifts; the kong path falls below the ground after 2 s), so no hips profile is drawn in the flight or in the run-out. It does NOT identify the flight height: the top surface height comes out at 0 m for the kong, so no measured obstacle height is used. A non-ballistic flight (a roll) cannot be detected by the residual (free hand anchors absorb it): the frontdive with and without its annotated roll window gives travel 1.88 m vs 1.80 m, so the travel uncertainty is about 0.1 m.
- Trunk tilt = angle of Hips->Head from +Z (ACU skeleton, pelvis-relative, 30 Hz). Inversion in the flight: none < 90 deg, mild 90-120 deg, severe > 120 deg. Trunk angular speed = max rate of change of that direction.
- Hand use: hands are on top when a hand is more than 0.3 m below the pelvis during the flight (reported). The hands score compares hand contacts in the flight on both sides (WD2 candidate run, ACU contacts on the top), since the WD2 top is not visible.
- Recovery: time from landing until the hips are at >= 85 % of their standing height for 0.25 s. Primary measure: hips = Foot height minus the lowest foot (pelvis-relative FK, no path needed). Fallback: the path hips.
- ACU cut = match-model play window (o + r * window, clip seconds) when measured; otherwise the clip length (optimistic, flagged). remaining = cut - ACU landing. The recovery test asks whether the body is upright-running before that cut (within 0.25 s counts half).
- Obstacle height class: the WD2 clip names carry a NNcm tag. It is used as an unverified hint only (the frontdive annotation puts its 100cm obstacle top at 1.0 m, which supports the reading); clips without a tag score neutral (0.5).
- Distance: WD2 travel (pelvis, takeoff->landing, path, WD2 +Y) vs ACU root displacement over the same flight. Ratio 0.8-1.25 scores full.
- Speed class: run-in body speed from the backward foot speed (>= 4.5 m/s sprint, >= 2.0 jog). This estimator reads about half the ACU sprint run-in speed: the ACU targets run 4-7 m/s (root displacement before takeoff, summary.json runin_speed_mps), the WD2 clips 1.0-3.4 m/s, so the speed classes are not comparable to ACU and the candidate contact band is biased on the slow-reading clips. Those clips are marked detection LOW; check their events by hand.
- Verdicts: strong fit = score >= 70, no severe inversion, upright by the cut (full recovery credit); possible = score >= 55; poor fit otherwise, and always poor when the timing score is below 0.3 (the flight is far from the ACU flight). The cut is the match-model play window; where it is not measured, a landing upright at once counts in full and anything later counts half. not analysable = no flight detected.
