- Lift points: non-finger body WITHOUT Foot / FootThumb1 (both sides). Feet are handled by the foot clamp IK only.
- L(f) = max_k(surface(y_k) + 0.01 - z_k), signed (not clamped at 0), on the blended pose.
- Upper bound U(f) on the plant (+-3 frames relaxed linearly): the largest lift dz for which both shoulders reach their hand targets within 0.95 x arm length (exact interval from the shoulder positions; hand target = x, y of the un-lifted hand at the first plant frame, y clamped, z = top + Hand offset). Off the plant U = +inf.
- D0 rule: 0 if L <= 0 <= U; L if L > 0; U if U < 0 and U >= L; L on conflict (U < L; penetration wins, touch fails).
- Smoothing: D = clip(gauss(D, sigma 5 frames), L, U) x 40, with U ignored where U < L. Taper towards 0 at the window ends with D >= L and D <= U kept (forced frames counted). Delta = (0, 0, D).
- Fades per clip: fi = min(0.20 s, 0.30 x window), fo = min(0.25 s, 0.35 x window). Pure WD2 part = clip time where the patch weight is 1, mapped to WD2 time through the time map.
- Hand IK (weight 1 while both hands keep their reach after the lift, 3-frame ramps) and foot clamp IK (lower of Foot / FootThumb1 raised to its support + offset, 3-frame ramps), applied before the crossfade.
- PASS = non-finger penetration (incl. feet after foot IK) <= 5 cm (box and ground, window) AND each hand within 5 cm of its target for >= 4 consecutive frames from the first plant frame AND largest Delta change <= 3 cm per 60 Hz frame. Fingers are reported separately. FAIL clips have `enabled: false`.
